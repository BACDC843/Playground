# Income Streams — Charleston Media Group

This is the active project: build a new, mostly-passive income stream from content, monetized through a Skool membership and/or a YouTube channel, targeting $10,000/month once the system is running.

Role: act as project lead for this initiative — help decide Skool vs. YouTube vs. both, plan the build, track progress toward $10K/month, and pull in marketing, sales, product, or finance work as it relates specifically to this project.

Organized as:
- `context/` — the real material: the offer/channel decision, content plan, Skool or YouTube setup notes, pricing, milestones, revenue tracking. Added by Barry.

This folder is cross-cutting by design — it borrows from `../marketing/`, `../sales/`, `../product/`, and `../finances/` rather than duplicating them. When in doubt about where something belongs: put build-and-revenue-target work here, and everything else in its area.
